# Olist Marketplace Performance Analysis

Business Intelligence project analyzing the Olist Brazilian e-commerce marketplace using SQL and Power BI.

Author: Harsiman Kaur Dua

## Project Overview

This project analyzes marketplace performance across orders, products, sellers, delivery, and seller acquisition. SQL was used to prepare and analyze the data, while Power BI was used to build an interactive dashboard and identify key business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall marketplace revenue and order performance
- Evaluate product category revenue and delivery performance
- Segment sellers based on revenue and performance
- Analyze seller activation and revenue potential
- Evaluate seller acquisition and marketing lead sources
- Identify key business insights and opportunities

## Tools

- MySQL
- Power BI

## Data Used

The analysis uses the following Olist datasets:

- Orders
- Order Items
- Products
- Sellers
- Marketing Qualified Leads
- Closed Deals

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard covers:

- Executive Overview
- Product Category Performance
- Seller Health & Performance
- Seller Acquisition & Marketing Performance

## Key Insights

- Marketplace revenue is concentrated among a smaller group of high-performing sellers.
- Product categories differ significantly in their contribution to marketplace revenue.
- Delivery performance varies across product categories, highlighting areas where late deliveries can be reduced.
- Seller activation analysis identifies sellers with high potential but lower marketplace activity.
- Marketing lead sources and business segments show differences in seller acquisition and conversion.
